# SupportFlow AI — Promotions, Coupons & Offers Policy

## Overview
SupportFlow provides various coupons, promotional codes, bank cashback offers, and premium membership (SupportFlow Gold/Pro) discounts. This document explains how promotional discounts work, common application issues, terms & conditions, and dispute resolution.

## Common Reasons Why Coupon Codes Fail

### 1. Minimum Order Value (MOV) Not Met
- Every coupon specifies a Minimum Order Value (excluding taxes, restaurant charges, and packaging fees).
- Example: Code `FEAST50` requires a subtotal of ₹299+. If food items subtotal is ₹280, the coupon will be rejected even if the final bill with taxes is ₹315.
- Solution: Add any small item or beverage to cross the subtotal threshold.

### 2. Coupon Expired or Usage Limit Reached
- Time expiration: Promo codes expire at 11:59 PM on their specified date.
- Frequency limit: Many codes are single-use per customer (e.g., `FIRSTORDER`, `WELCOME100`) or once per week.
- Platform cap: Flash sales or high-discount promo codes often have total redemption limits (e.g., first 5,000 customers).

### 3. Restaurant-Specific Exclusion
- Certain partner restaurants (such as select fine dining brands or outlet franchises) opt out of general platform discounts.
- Look for the "Not eligible for coupons" badge under the restaurant menu details.

### 4. Payment Method Incompatibility
- Bank-specific promo codes (e.g., `HDFC20`, `ICICIFEST`, `CRED50`) only apply when you select the exact eligible payment method (credit card, debit card, or UPI) at the checkout stage.
- If you change your payment method at checkout, the applied coupon is automatically detached.

### 5. Account Ineligibility
- New user coupons (`WELCOME50`, `TASTYFIRST`) are valid strictly on the first completed order for verified phone numbers and unique device IDs.
- Creating multiple accounts using the same phone, email, or device triggers fraud detection and revokes eligibility.

## SupportFlow Gold / Pro Membership Benefits

### Benefits
- **Free Delivery**: Zero delivery charges on orders above ₹199 from all partner restaurants within 10 km.
- **VIP Priority Escalation**: 24/7 dedicated priority support routing.
- **Exclusive Discounts**: Up to 30% extra off at premium partner restaurants.
- **On-time Guarantee**: Get ₹50 coupon in your wallet if order is delayed past the delivery estimate.

### Membership Renewal & Cancellation
- Auto-renewal can be disabled anytime under Profile → SupportFlow Gold → Manage Membership.
- Instant refunds for Gold plans are eligible within 48 hours of purchase if no Gold benefits (free delivery or discounts) were utilized.

## Cashbacks & Bank Discounts

### Bank Card Cashbacks
- Bank cashbacks are credited directly to your bank account or credit card statement within 3 to 7 business days, depending on the issuing bank.
- Wallet cashbacks (Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay) are credited to your digital wallet within 24 hours of successful order delivery.

## Compensation Policy for Valid Missed Discounts
If a verified technical glitch prevented a valid promo code from applying:
1. Customer must report the issue within 24 hours of placing the order.
2. Share screenshot of the error message or coupon details.
3. Our customer agent will verify the order value and eligibility.
4. If eligible, the discount amount is credited directly to SupportFlow Wallet within 2 hours or refunded to the original payment source within 3-5 business days.
