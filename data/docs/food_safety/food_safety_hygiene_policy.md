# SupportFlow AI — Food Safety, Hygiene & Quality Policy

## Overview
SupportFlow adheres to strict food safety guidelines in partnership with national food safety authorities (FSSAI) and our restaurant kitchen audit partners. We treat foreign objects, food poisoning, spoilage, and severe hygiene lapses with highest urgency and zero tolerance.

## Critical Food Safety Incidents

### 1. Foreign Object Found in Food
- **Examples**: Glass, plastic, metal shard, insects, hair, wire, stones.
- **Immediate Customer Protocol**:
  1. Stop eating the food immediately.
  2. Do NOT throw away the food, container, or the foreign object.
  3. Take clear, well-lit photos and a short video showing the item in the food container and restaurant packaging.
  4. Submit a ticket via the app under Help → Orders → Food Quality & Safety → Foreign Object.
- **SupportFlow Action**:
  - Full refund processed immediately to original payment method or wallet.
  - Complimentary compensatory coupon issued for future orders.
  - Restaurant kitchen is flagged for mandatory sanitation inspection.
  - Escalated directly to High Priority Human Safety Desk.

### 2. Suspected Food Poisoning / Sickness
- **Symptoms**: Acute nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps, diarrhea, fever within 2–24 hours of meal consumption.
- **Resolution & Assistance**:
  - Immediate human escalation with dedicated emergency support executive.
  - Complete order refund issued unconditionally.
  - Our customer safety team covers verified outpatient medical expenses upon submission of a doctor's consultation receipt and prescription.
  - Audit team temporarily pauses online ordering from the flagged outlet until kitchen swabs and batch samples are cleared.

### 3. Spoiled, Rotten or Undercooked Food
- **Spoilage**: Foul smell, sour/rancid taste, curdled dairy, mould/fungus growth.
- **Undercooked Meat/Fish/Eggs**: Raw center, pink poultry, translucent pork/chicken.
- **Resolution**: Full refund of the affected dishes or entire order, plus immediate replacement if customer prefers.

### 4. Vegetarian / Non-Vegetarian Cross Contamination & Allergen Violations
- Delivering non-vegetarian food to a customer who ordered pure vegetarian or vegan food is a critical protocol violation.
- Failing to observe declared allergens (peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, dairy, gluten) despite kitchen instructions.
- **Remedy**: Instant 100% refund, formal apology, platform credit, and penalty levied on the restaurant.

## Quality Assurance & Kitchen Audits
- Restaurants with 2 or more food safety complaints in a 7-day period are automatically delisted pending on-site health inspection.
- FSSAI hygiene ratings must be displayed by restaurants on their digital storefront.
- Tamper-evident seals are mandatory for all delivery packaging to prevent transit contamination.
