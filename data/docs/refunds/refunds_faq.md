## Refund Policy Overview

**Our Refund Guarantee:**
- **Missing items:** Full refund for any item not delivered
- **Wrong items:** Full refund or re-delivery for completely wrong orders
- **Quality issues:** Partial or full refund at our discretion (with photo proof)
- **Late cancellations:** Partial refund depending on restaurant preparation status
- **App/technical errors:** Full refund if order failed due to our system

**Timeline:** Refunds are processed within **3-5 business days** to original payment method, or **instantly** to SupportFlow Wallet.

---

## How to Request a Refund

### Problem: Customer wants to request a refund

**Solution:**
1. Go to **My Orders → [Order] → Report Issue**
2. Select the issue type:
   - Missing items → select the specific items
   - Wrong order → describe what you received
   - Food quality → attach a photo
   - Order not received → confirm delivery address
3. Submit the request — you will receive a confirmation email
4. Our team reviews requests within **24 hours**
5. You will be notified of the decision via email and app notification

**Note:** Refund requests must be submitted within **48 hours** of delivery. Requests submitted after this window may not be accepted.

---

## How to Cancel an Order

### Problem: Customer wants to cancel their order

**Solution and Cancellation Policy:**

| Order Stage | Refund |
|---|---|
| Order Placed (not yet confirmed by restaurant) | Full refund |
| Restaurant confirmed, not yet prepared | Full refund |
| Restaurant preparing the order | Partial refund (delivery fee and service charges may apply) |
| Out for delivery | No refund (order cannot be cancelled) |
| Delivered | No refund (use Report Issue instead) |

**Steps:**
1. Go to **My Orders → [Order] → Cancel Order**
2. Select a cancellation reason
3. Confirm cancellation — refund timeline depends on order stage

## Missing Items in Delivery

### Problem: Some items were not in the delivery bag

**Solution:**
1. Report missing items within 48 hours via **My Orders → Report Issue → Missing Items**
2. Select which items were missing from the order list
3. We will issue a **full refund for missing items** to your original payment method or wallet
4. We do not offer re-delivery for missing items from completed orders

---

## Wrong Items Received

### Problem: Received completely different items than ordered

**Solution:**
1. Report within 48 hours via **My Orders → Report Issue → Wrong Items**
2. Attach a photo if possible
3. For completely wrong orders, we offer:
   - Full refund to original payment method, OR
   - Store credit (processed faster)
4. For partially wrong orders, we refund only the incorrect items

---

## Food Quality Issues

### Problem: Food arrived cold, spoiled, or inedible

**Solution:**
1. Report within 2 hours of delivery via **My Orders → Report Issue → Food Quality**
2. Attach a clear photo showing the issue
3. Our team reviews and may offer:
   - Partial refund (10-50% depending on severity)
   - Full refund for inedible/unsafe food
   - SupportFlow credits as compensation
4. We take food safety very seriously and will flag the restaurant
