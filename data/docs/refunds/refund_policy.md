# SupportFlow AI — Sensible Restaurant & Situation Refund Policy

## 1. Overview
SupportFlow AI's refund policy is grounded in fair, transparent, and balanced platform standards. 
We **do not require customers to upload photographs**. Photos introduce friction, cannot capture temperature or missing items, and are often ambiguous. Instead, issues are resolved conversationally and interactively based on:
1. **The Specific Situation & Severity** (complete meal ruin vs. minor condiment leak, missing item, temperature drop, wrong item, dietary breach).
2. **Restaurant & Cuisine Category Rules** (Biryani packaging, Pizza crust integrity, Ice cream zero-melt guarantee, Pure Veg dietary sanctity).
3. **Telemetry & Order Details** (Rider transit times, database item pricing, preparation statuses).

---

## 2. Situation & Restaurant-Specific Resolution Matrix

### A. Packaging & Spillage Issues
- **Severe Total Damage / Inedible (Gravy soaked entire meal, broken box, beverage flooded food)**:
  - Applicable to all restaurants (e.g. *Meghana Foods*, *Domino's*, *Haldiram's*, *Burger King*).
  - **Resolution**: **100% Full Refund** credited to SupportFlow Wallet instantly or original payment method, OR a 100% meal reorder credit.
- **Minor / Partial Leak (Side raita, mirchi ka salan, sambhar, dip, or sauce container leaked)**:
  - Main course (e.g., biryani handi, pizza, burger, dosa) is intact and fully edible.
  - **Resolution**: **25% to 30% Courtesy Wallet Credit** OR **₹40–₹60 Next-Order Condiment Voucher** (`SALAN50`, `CHUTNEY40`).

### B. Temperature & Freshness Issues
- **Hot Food Arrived Cold / Lukewarm (Biryani, Pizza, Curries, Burgers)**:
  - Food temperature drops naturally during courier transit across city traffic. The meal remains freshly prepared and completely safe.
  - **Resolution**: **20% to 25% Courtesy Wallet Credit** OR **₹75 Apology Voucher** (`WARM25`), plus restaurant-specific reheating instructions (e.g., covered skillet crisping for pizza, moist microwave for biryani).
  - *Full 100% refund is not awarded solely for temperature drop.*
- **Frozen Desserts & Ice Cream Melted (Zero-Melt Guarantee)**:
  - For restaurants like *Corner House*, *Naturals Ice Cream*, *Theobroma*, *Baskin Robbins*:
  - Melted ice cream or warm thickshakes permanently lose texture and cannot be refrozen.
  - **Resolution**: **100% Full Item Refund** or Free Replacement Credit (`MELTFREE`).

### C. Missing Items
- When specific dishes, sides, or beverages are missing:
  - **Resolution**: **Exact Item-Level Refund** (100% of the missing dish's price based on SQLite receipt) + **₹30 Delivery Inconvenience Credit**.
  - We do not cancel or refund the entire order if only a side or add-on is absent.

### D. Wrong Order & Dietary Violations
- **Dietary Sanctity Violation (CRITICAL: Non-Veg delivered on a Vegetarian order)**:
  - Zero tolerance policy across all partner restaurants, especially Pure Veg partners (*Haldiram's*, *Sagar Ratna*).
  - **Resolution**: **100% Full Order Refund** + **₹200 Apology Dining Voucher** (`RESPECT200`) + Escalated restaurant audit ticket.
- **Wrong Dish Delivered (Different dish or topping mismatch)**:
  - **Resolution**: **100% Dish Refund** or Instant Replacement Voucher (`REPLACE100`). Customer may keep the delivered dish.

### E. Culinary Quality & Food Safety
- **Burnt, Charred, Inedible, or Foreign Contaminant**:
  - **Resolution**: **100% Full Refund** + Restaurant supervisor investigation.
- **Taste Dissatisfaction (Subjective: too spicy / too mild)**:
  - **Resolution**: **₹50 Gourmet Discovery Voucher** (`FLAVOR50`). No cash refund.

### F. Delivery Delays (GPS Verified)
- **30 to 45 minutes beyond estimated arrival**: **₹50 On-Time Voucher** (`ONTIME50`).
- **45 to 60 minutes delay**: **35% Partial Refund** or ₹120 wallet credit.
- **60+ minutes delay**: **50% to 100% Refund** depending on whether customer accepted the meal.

### G. Ineligible Claims (0% Refund)
- Customer cancellation after the restaurant starts cooking (compensates restaurant partner).
- Incorrect delivery address provided by customer.
- Claims submitted beyond 24 hours of delivery.

## 3. Interactive Customer Settlement Methods
Customers can select their preferred compensation in real-time during chat:
1. **⚡ Instant SupportFlow Wallet Credit**: Credited immediately with live transaction ID.
2. **🎟️ Apology Dining Voucher**: High-value promo code ready for the next order.
3. **💳 Original Payment Method**: Initiated to original bank/card/UPI (3–5 business days).
