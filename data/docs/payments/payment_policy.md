# SupportFlow AI — Payment & Billing Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, all UPI apps)
- Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay)
- Debit Cards (all Indian bank debit cards)
- SupportFlow Wallet (in-app wallet with instant refund capability)
- Net Banking (all major Indian banks)
- Cash on Delivery (selected areas and restaurants only)

## How Payments Work
1. Payment is authorized (held) when you place the order
2. Payment is captured (charged) only when the restaurant confirms your order
3. If the restaurant rejects your order, the authorization is released within 2-4 hours (no actual charge)

## Duplicate Charge / Double Debit Issues

### What looks like a duplicate charge but isn't:
- Some banks show a "pending" authorization AND the actual charge simultaneously
- The authorization auto-reverses within 24-48 hours — you were not actually charged twice
- Check your final statement (not pending transactions) after 48 hours

### If it IS a confirmed duplicate charge:
- The extra amount will be automatically refunded within 3-5 business days
- Contact support with your bank statement showing both charges
- We will manually raise a refund with your bank within 24 hours of confirmation
- You will receive an SMS and email confirmation once the refund is initiated

## Payment Failure / Order Not Placed
If your payment was deducted but no order was created:
- This is typically a network timeout issue during payment
- The amount is usually auto-reversed within 24-48 hours
- If not reversed after 48 hours, contact support with your transaction ID
- We will coordinate with your bank to expedite the reversal

## Charges Breakdown
Your order bill includes:
- Food items subtotal
- Delivery fee (distance-based, shown before checkout)
- Platform service fee (small %)
- GST (5% on restaurant food, 18% on delivery fee in India)
- Optional tips for delivery partner

## Invoice and Billing
- GST invoices are automatically generated for every order
- Download invoice: Orders → Select Order → Download Invoice
- Invoices are retained for 2 years
- For business expense claims, contact support for a batch invoice export

## Security — What We Never Ask For
- We NEVER ask for your full card number
- We NEVER ask for your CVV or OTP over chat
- We NEVER ask for your bank password
- Only share the last 4 digits of your card if needed for verification
- All payments go through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways

## SupportFlow Wallet
- Top up via UPI, card, or net banking
- Wallet balance never expires
- Instant refunds to wallet (no waiting period)
- Cannot be transferred to bank (except via formal refund process)
- Use wallet for faster checkout — no OTP required for wallet payments
