## Payment Methods Accepted

**Solution:** SupportFlow accepts:
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay)
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
- Net banking
- SupportFlow Wallet
- Cash on Delivery (available in select areas)

**Note:** We do NOT accept prepaid cards or international cards from unsupported regions.

---

## Payment Failed / Order Not Placed

### Problem: Payment was deducted but order was not confirmed

**Solution:**
1. Wait 5-10 minutes — sometimes there is a processing delay
2. Check your bank statement or UPI app for the transaction status
3. If deducted and order not placed, the amount will auto-reverse within **5-7 business days**
4. If auto-reversal doesn't happen, contact support with your transaction ID and bank reference number

**Policy:** We do not manually initiate reversals for transactions not visible in our system. Your bank processes reversals automatically.

## Charged Twice for the Same Order

### Problem: Duplicate charge on customer's account

**Solution:**
1. Go to **My Orders → Order Details → View Invoice** to verify charges
2. Check if you placed two separate orders accidentally
3. If genuinely charged twice for one order, contact support with:
   - Order ID
   - Bank statement screenshot showing both charges
4. Duplicate charges are resolved within **3-5 business days** with a full refund

## Promo Codes and Discounts Not Applied

### Problem: Coupon or promo code did not work

**Solution:**
1. Ensure the promo code is applied **before** placing the order — codes cannot be applied after payment
2. Check the terms: most codes have minimum order value requirements, specific restaurant restrictions, or expiry dates
3. If the code is valid but not applying, log out and log back in, then try again
4. If the issue persists, contact support with the promo code — we will manually credit the discount if applicable

---

## How to Get Invoice / Receipt

### Problem: Customer needs a GST invoice or payment receipt

**Solution:**
1. Go to **My Orders → [Order] → View Invoice**
2. Tap **Download PDF** or **Email Invoice** to get a copy
3. For GST invoices (business purchases), enter your GSTIN in **Account → Business Settings → GST Details**
4. Invoices are available for all completed orders for up to 12 months

---

## SupportFlow Wallet

### Problem: Questions about wallet balance, top-up, or refunds to wallet

**Solution:**
- Add money to wallet via **Account → SupportFlow Wallet → Add Money**
- Wallet balance does not expire and is fully refundable upon account closure
- Refunds can be issued to wallet (instant) or original payment method (3-5 business days)
- Wallet cannot be used for Cash on Delivery orders
