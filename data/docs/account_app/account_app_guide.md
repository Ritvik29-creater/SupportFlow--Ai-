# SupportFlow AI — Account & App Support Guide

## Account Access Issues

### Forgot Password
1. Open the SupportFlow app
2. Tap "Login" → "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered mobile number
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP (valid for 10 minutes)
5. Set new password: minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character
6. Tap "Reset Password" → you're done!

**OTP not received?**
- Check SMS inbox and spam folder
- Wait 2 minutes and tap "Resend OTP"
- Ensure mobile number is correct (check country code)
- If still not received: try the "Call me with OTP" option

### Account Locked/Blocked

**Reasons accounts get blocked:**
- 5+ failed login attempts (auto-block for 1 hour)
- Suspicious login from new device/location
- Policy violation detected

**How to unlock:**
- Wait 1 hour for auto-unlock (failed login block)
- For policy violation: check your registered email for details
- For suspicious activity: verify identity via OTP and then change password
- If email access lost: contact support with government ID

### Can't Log In (Other Reasons)
- Ensure you're using the correct email/phone
- Check if CAPS LOCK is on for password
- Clear browser cache or app cache
- Try logging in from a different device to isolate app vs account issue

## App Technical Issues

### App Crashes / Not Loading
**Quick fixes (try in order):**
1. Force close the app and reopen
2. Check your internet connection (try WiFi and mobile data)
3. Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → SupportFlow → Storage → Clear Cache
4. Update the app to the latest version (check Play Store / App Store)
5. Restart your phone
6. Reinstall the app (your account data is safe in the cloud)

### App Running Slowly
- Close other background apps
- Free up phone storage (app needs at least 500MB free)
- Clear app cache
- Ensure you have Android 8+ or iOS 14+

### Payment Screen Not Loading
- Check internet connection — payments require stable connection
- Disable VPN if active (VPNs can interfere with payment gateway)
- Try a different payment method
- Clear app cache and retry

## Profile Management

### Changing Phone Number
1. Settings → Account → Phone Number → Edit
2. Verify OLD number with OTP
3. Enter new number
4. Verify NEW number with OTP
5. Change confirmed

**If old number is inaccessible:**
- Submit manual verification request
- Provide government ID (Aadhaar/Passport)
- Processing: 2–3 business days

### Changing Delivery Address
1. Go to your profile → "Saved Addresses"
2. Tap the address you want to edit, or "Add New Address"
3. Search or pin your location on the map
4. Add house number/floor/landmark details
5. Save as Home, Work, or Other

### Deleting Account
- Settings → Privacy → Delete My Account
- Warning: This is permanent — all order history will be lost
- Any pending refunds will be processed to your original payment method first
- Takes 30 days to fully process

## Notifications & Communications

### Not Receiving Order Notifications
1. Check: Settings → Notifications → SupportFlow → Allow All
2. Ensure "Do Not Disturb" mode is not active
3. Check battery optimization is disabled for SupportFlow
4. Reinstall if issue persists

### Receiving Too Many Emails/SMS
- Settings → Communication Preferences → Unsubscribe from promotional emails
- "Transactional" messages (order updates, payment receipts) cannot be unsubscribed

## Data & Privacy

### Downloading Your Data
- Settings → Privacy → Download My Data
- You'll receive an email with your data within 48 hours

### Reporting Unauthorized Account Access
1. Immediately change your password
2. Go to Settings → Security → "Active Sessions" → End all other sessions
3. Enable two-factor authentication
4. Contact support with your account details

## Security Best Practices
⚠️ SupportFlow will NEVER ask for your password via chat or phone
⚠️ OTPs are only sent to your registered number — never share with anyone claiming to be support
⚠️ Enable biometric login (fingerprint/face ID) for account security
⚠️ Review active sessions regularly in Security settings
