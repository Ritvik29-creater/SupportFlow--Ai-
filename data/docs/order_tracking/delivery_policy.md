# SupportFlow AI — Order Tracking & Delivery Policy

## Order Statuses Explained
- **Order Placed**: Your order has been received and sent to the restaurant
- **Confirmed**: Restaurant has accepted your order and started preparing
- **Preparing**: Kitchen is actively preparing your food
- **Ready for Pickup**: Food is ready, waiting for a delivery partner
- **Out for Delivery**: Delivery partner has picked up your order and is on the way
- **Delivered**: Order has been delivered to the provided address

## Estimated Delivery Times
- Standard delivery: 30-45 minutes from order placement
- Peak hours (12-2 PM, 7-10 PM): 45-60 minutes
- Stormy/rainy weather: Add 15-30 minutes to estimated time
- Long distance (>5 km): Add 10-15 minutes

## What to Do if Your Order is Delayed

### Delay of 15-30 minutes beyond ETA:
- Check the live tracking map in the app for real-time location
- The restaurant may be experiencing high volume — this is normal
- No action needed yet; delivery partner is on the way

### Delay of 30-60 minutes beyond ETA:
- Use the in-app "Contact Driver" button to call the delivery partner
- If unreachable, use "Contact Restaurant" to check order status
- You may be eligible for a partial refund or wallet credit as compensation

### Delay of more than 60 minutes beyond ETA:
- You are automatically eligible for a full refund or re-delivery
- Contact support and we will immediately escalate to our delivery ops team
- If the order is still not delivered, it will be marked as "non-delivery" and fully refunded

## Missing Items
If items are missing from your delivery:
1. Take a photo of what you received immediately
2. Go to Orders → Report Missing Items
3. Select the specific missing items
4. Choose: refund for missing items OR re-delivery (if restaurant is nearby)
5. Resolution is typically within 30 minutes

## Wrong Order Delivered
If you received someone else's order or completely wrong items:
- Do NOT eat the food (for safety reasons)
- Report immediately via the app
- You are eligible for a full refund OR a fresh re-delivery from the same restaurant
- The delivery partner and restaurant will both be notified

## Order Cancellation Policy
- **Before restaurant accepts**: Cancel freely, full refund in 2 hours
- **After restaurant accepts but before preparation starts**: Cancel with 80% refund (20% goes to restaurant for preparation costs)
- **During preparation**: Cancellation may not be possible; contact support for assistance
- **Once out for delivery**: Cannot be cancelled

## Driver Contact Information
- In-app call button is available once your order is "Out for Delivery"
- For privacy, phone numbers are masked — calls go through our secure network
- If the driver is unreachable for 10+ minutes while "Out for Delivery", contact support immediately
