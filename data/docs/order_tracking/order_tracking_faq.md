## How to Track Your Order

### Problem: Customer wants to know where their order is

**Solution:**
1. Open the SupportFlow app and go to **My Orders**
2. Tap your active order to see real-time tracking on the map
3. You will see the current status: Order Placed → Restaurant Confirmed → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered
4. The estimated delivery time (ETA) updates automatically based on GPS tracking

**Note:** Live tracking is available once the driver picks up your order. Before that, you will see the restaurant's preparation status.

---

## Delayed Orders

### Problem: Order is taking longer than expected

**Solution:**
1. Check the app for the latest ETA — delays can happen due to high demand, traffic, or restaurant preparation time
2. If your ETA has passed by more than 15 minutes with no update, contact support
3. We will contact the driver and restaurant on your behalf
4. If the order is unreasonably delayed (over 30 minutes past ETA), you may be eligible for a partial refund or delivery fee waiver

**Policy:** If an order is cancelled due to excessive delay, you will receive a full refund within 3-5 business days.

---

## Missing Delivery

### Problem: App shows delivered but customer did not receive order

**Solution:**
1. Check nearby areas, neighbors, building lobby, or mailroom
2. Check if the delivery photo (if provided) matches your location
3. Report the issue within **48 hours** of the delivery timestamp
4. Go to **My Orders → [Order] → Report Issue → Order Not Received**
5. Our team will investigate and issue a full refund if confirmed

---

## Wrong or Incomplete Address

### Problem: Order was delivered to wrong address

**Solution:**
1. You cannot change a delivery address once the restaurant has confirmed the order
2. Before ordering, always verify your saved address in **Account → Saved Addresses**
3. If the driver has not yet picked up the order, contact support immediately — we may be able to update it
4. Orders delivered to wrong addresses due to customer error may not qualify for a full refund

---

## Contacting Your Driver

### Problem: Customer needs to reach their delivery driver

**Solution:**
1. Open the active order in the app
2. Tap **Contact Driver** to call or message them through our anonymized calling system
3. Your real phone number is never shared with the driver
4. Driver contact is only available while the order is active
