# SupportFlow AI — Delivery Partner Complaint Policy

## Overview
SupportFlow takes delivery partner behavior very seriously. We maintain strict standards for all delivery partners on our platform. Customer safety and dignity are non-negotiable.

## Reporting a Delivery Partner Issue

### How to Report During an Active Order
1. Go to your active order tracking screen
2. Tap the "..." menu → "Report an Issue with Driver"
3. Select the issue type
4. Add description and any evidence (photo/video)

### How to Report After Delivery
1. Go to Orders → select the completed order
2. Tap "Rate & Review" → select "Delivery Partner Experience"
3. Choose the issue type from the dropdown
4. Provide detailed description

## Severity Levels and Response Times

### 🔴 Critical Safety Issues — Response within 30 minutes
- Physical assault, threat, or aggression
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate behavior
- Delivery partner under influence of substances
- Demanding bribes or extra cash
- Theft of food or property

**Actions Taken:**
- Immediate account suspension pending investigation
- Safety team review within 1 hour
- Police coordination if customer requests it
- Full refund + ₹200 compensation credit
- Partner permanently banned if confirmed

### 🟠 Serious Misconduct — Response within 2 hours
- Extreme verbal abuse or threats
- Refusal to deliver to doorstep without valid reason
- Delivered to wrong address despite correction
- Deliberately damaged packaging

**Actions Taken:**
- Account flagged and under review
- Formal investigation within 24 hours
- Full refund if delivery was affected
- Partner faces suspension or deactivation

### 🟡 Standard Issues — Response within 4 hours
- Rude or dismissive behavior (non-threatening)
- Delivery partner not responding to calls/chat
- Minor wrong delivery
- Slow delivery without communication

**Actions Taken:**
- Feedback logged on partner's profile
- Performance review triggered
- Compensation credit ₹30–₹75 based on impact

## What Information Helps Your Complaint
- Delivery partner's name (visible in order screen during delivery)
- Partner's vehicle number (if visible)
- Screenshot of the chat/conversation
- Timestamp of the incident
- Any photos or videos (for safety incidents)

## Your Rights as a Customer
- You are NEVER required to engage with a rude or threatening delivery partner
- You can request a different partner for future deliveries from the same area
- You have the right to refuse delivery if the partner's behavior is unacceptable
- For safety threats: contact local police and then inform us

## Compensation Policy for Partner Misconduct

| Severity | Refund | Additional Compensation |
|----------|--------|------------------------|
| Critical | 100% full refund | ₹200 safety compensation credit |
| Serious | 100% full refund | ₹100 inconvenience credit |
| Moderate | Partial if applicable | ₹30–₹75 goodwill credit |
| Minor | Case-by-case | ₹20–₹30 goodwill credit |

## Safety Resources
- For immediate danger: Call 100 (Police) or 112 (Emergency)
- Our 24/7 safety helpline is available via the app for serious incidents
- We partner with local police departments for serious misconduct cases
