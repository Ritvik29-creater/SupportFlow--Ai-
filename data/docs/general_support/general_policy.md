# SupportFlow AI — General Support & Account Help

## Account Issues

### Login Problems
- Forgot password: Use "Forgot Password" on the login screen — OTP sent to registered phone
- Account locked: After 5 failed login attempts, account is locked for 30 minutes
- Unable to receive OTP: Check if your phone number is correct; contact support for manual verification
- Account deactivated: Contact support with your registered email to reactivate

### Profile Update
- Change phone number: Requires OTP verification on both old and new number
- Change email: Requires clicking a confirmation link sent to the new email
- Change delivery address: Can add/edit in Profile → Saved Addresses

## App Issues

### App Crashes or Won't Load
1. Force close the app and reopen
2. Check your internet connection (use Wi-Fi or 4G/5G)
3. Clear app cache: Phone Settings → Apps → SupportFlow → Clear Cache
4. Update the app to the latest version from Play Store / App Store
5. Uninstall and reinstall if the problem persists

### Location/GPS Issues
- Make sure location permissions are granted to the app
- Enable "Precise Location" (not just "Approximate") for accurate delivery
- If your address isn't found, use the map pin to drop your location manually

### Notification Issues
- Check notification permissions for the app
- Check if Do Not Disturb mode is enabled
- Important order updates are also sent via SMS as backup

## Referral & Promo Codes

### How Referral Codes Work
- Share your referral code with friends
- When they place their first order (min. ₹200), you both get ₹50 credit
- Referral credit is added to SupportFlow Wallet within 24 hours of friend's delivery
- Maximum 20 referrals per account per month

### Promo Code Issues
- Promo not applying: Check minimum order value, applicable restaurants, and expiry date
- Promo already used: Most promos are single-use per account
- Can't stack promos: Only one promo code per order (wallet cashback can still apply)

## Subscription / SupportFlow Pro

### SupportFlow Pro Benefits
- Free delivery on all orders above ₹99 (no delivery fee)
- Priority customer support (response within 30 minutes)
- Exclusive restaurant discounts (up to 30% off)
- Early access to new restaurants and features

### Pro Subscription Issues
- If Pro benefits are not applying, log out and log back in
- Refund for unused Pro months: Contact support within 7 days of billing
- Auto-renewal cancellation: Profile → Subscriptions → Cancel Auto-Renew

## Escalation to Human Support
When our AI cannot fully resolve your issue, it will automatically create a human support ticket.

### Human support availability:
- **Chat support**: 9 AM - 11 PM IST, 7 days a week
- **Phone support**: 9 AM - 9 PM IST (Pro subscribers only)
- **Email support**: 24/7 (response within 4 hours during business hours)
- **Emergency (safety issues)**: 24/7 hotline available

### What to expect after escalation:
1. You receive a ticket ID (e.g., TKT-12345)
2. A human agent reviews your case and conversation history
3. You're notified via SMS + app notification when the agent responds
4. Most cases resolved within 2-4 hours during business hours

### Tips to get faster resolution:
- Provide your order ID if available
- Describe exactly what went wrong in detail
- Include photos if it's a food quality or packaging issue
- Mention the restaurant name and approximate order time

## Privacy & Data

- We use AI to understand your queries but humans review escalated cases
- You can request deletion of your account data: Privacy Settings → Request Data Deletion
