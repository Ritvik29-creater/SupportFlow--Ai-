## Account Login Issues

### Problem: Customer cannot log in to their account

**Solution:**
1. Ensure you are using the correct email/phone number registered with SupportFlow
2. Try **Forgot Password** → enter your email → check inbox (and spam folder) for reset link
3. For phone-number login, ensure you have signal to receive the OTP
4. If OTP is not received, tap **Resend OTP** after 60 seconds
5. If you still cannot log in, contact support with your registered email — we will verify your identity

---

## Updating Profile Information

### Problem: Customer wants to update name, phone, email, or address

**Solution:**
1. Go to **Account → Profile → Edit Profile**
2. You can update: name, profile photo, email (requires email verification), and phone number (requires OTP verification)
3. To add or edit delivery addresses: **Account → Saved Addresses → Add/Edit**
4. Email changes require re-verification for security

---

## App Not Working / Crashing

### Problem: App crashes, freezes, or certain features not working

**Solution:**
1. Force-close the app and reopen it
2. Check your internet connection
3. Update the app to the latest version from the Play Store or App Store
4. Clear app cache: **Settings → Apps → SupportFlow → Clear Cache**
5. If the issue persists, restart your phone
6. Still not working? Uninstall and reinstall the app (your account data is saved in the cloud)
7. Report persistent bugs via **Account → Help → Report a Bug** with your device model and OS version

---

## Push Notifications Not Working

### Problem: Customer is not receiving order update notifications

**Solution:**
1. Go to your phone's **Settings → Notifications → SupportFlow** and ensure notifications are enabled
2. In the app: **Account → Notifications → Order Updates** — ensure all toggles are on
3. Check if Do Not Disturb mode is blocking notifications
4. Reinstalling the app refreshes notification permissions

---

## Deleting Account

### Problem: Customer wants to delete their account

**Solution:**
1. Go to **Account → Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**
2. You will be asked to confirm — all order history, saved addresses, and wallet balance will be permanently deleted
3. Wallet balance will be refunded to your original payment method before deletion (takes 3-5 business days)
4. Once deleted, the account cannot be recovered

**Note:** If you have active orders, you must wait for them to complete before deleting your account.

---

## Referral Program

### Problem: Customer has questions about the referral program

**Solution:**
1. Find your referral code in **Account → Refer & Earn**
2. Share your code with friends — when they place their first order, both you and your friend receive a discount
3. Referral credits are applied automatically to your next order
4. Referral bonuses expire 30 days after being earned
5. Referral abuse (using multiple accounts) will result in all credits being voided and possible account suspension
