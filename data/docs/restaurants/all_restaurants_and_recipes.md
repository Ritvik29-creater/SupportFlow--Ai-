## 2. Authentic Culinary Recipes & Ingredients

### Hyderabadi Dum Biryani
- **Key Ingredients**: Aged Basmati rice, chicken/mutton marinated in curd, fried onions (birista), Kashmiri saffron in warm milk, desi ghee, mint, shahi jeera, star anise, cardamom.
- **Preparation Method**: Par-boil basmati rice with whole aromatics to 70%. Alternate layers of marinated meat and fragrant rice. Drizzle saffron milk, mint, and pure ghee. Seal airtight with dough ('Dum') and slow-cook on low flame for 45 minutes.

### Neapolitan Margherita D.O.C Pizza
- **Key Ingredients**: Italian Tipo 00 flour (24-48hr cold fermentation), crushed San Marzano D.O.P tomatoes, fresh buffalo mozzarella (fior di latte), Genovese basil, cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, sea salt.
- **Baking Method**: Hand-stretched dough baked in a wood-fired dome oven at 450°C for 60 to 90 seconds. Features airy, blistered, leopard-spotted charred crust (cornicione).

### Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken)
- **Key Ingredients**: Boneless tandoor-charred chicken thighs, ripe plum tomato reduction, salted butter, cashew paste, double cream, sun-dried kasuri methi (fenugreek leaves), degi mirch.
- **Preparation Method**: Skewered tandoor chicken folded into strained velvet tomato-cashew makhani gravy, finished with toasted crushed fenugreek and butter.

### Dal Makhani (Bukhara Style)
- **Key Ingredients**: Whole black urad dal, red rajma beans, ginger-garlic paste, fresh tomato puree, white butter (makhan), dairy cream, Kashmiri chilli.
- **Preparation Method**: Slow-simmered over low charcoal fire for 16-18 hours until naturally creamy. Infused with charcoal smoke ('Dhungar technique').

## 3. Post-Delivery Food Quality & Complaint Policies

When food has been delivered, customers have a **2-hour window** to report issues:
1. **Food Arrived Cold**:
   - Eligible for **100% refund** of the affected items.
   - Option A: Instant credit to **SupportFlow Wallet** (immediate, usable on any restaurant).
   - Option B: Refund to **original payment source** (UPI, credit/debit card: 5-7 business days).
   - Additional ₹50 goodwill apology voucher applied to account.

2. **Missing or Incorrect Items**:
   - Immediate 100% refund of missing dish amount.
   - Instant re-order dispatch option without extra delivery fee.

3. **Spilled / Damaged Packaging**:
   - If food leaked or container was crushed, 100% full refund is approved immediately.

4. **Food Safety / Hygiene Violation (Foreign Object, Spoilage, Illness)**:
   - Immediate 100% full order refund + ₹200 safety compensation credit.
   - Restaurant receives immediate formal quality audit notice.
   - Ticket escalated with high urgency (`TKT-XXXX`) to Human Escalation Lead.
