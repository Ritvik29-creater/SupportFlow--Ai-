## Why Is a Restaurant Unavailable?

### Problem: Restaurant shows as unavailable or closed

**Solution:** Restaurants may be unavailable because:
1. **Outside operating hours** — check the restaurant's listed hours in the app
2. **High demand** — restaurants can pause accepting orders during peak times
3. **Temporary closure** — for maintenance, holidays, or special events
4. **Delivery zone** — the restaurant may not deliver to your current location
5. **App-level pause** — the restaurant may have technical issues with our platform

**Tip:** Add the restaurant to your **Favourites** to get notified when it comes back online.

## Allergen and Dietary Information

### Problem: Customer needs allergen or dietary info before ordering

**Solution:**
1. Tap on any menu item to see the full **Ingredient & Allergen** section
2. Filter restaurants by dietary preference:
   - **Vegan** — no animal products
   - **Vegetarian** — no meat/fish
   - **Jain** — no root vegetables, no eggs
   - **Gluten-free** — items without wheat, barley, rye
3. Look for the **allergen badge** (🌰 nuts, 🥚 eggs, 🐟 fish, 🥛 dairy) on menu items
4. For serious allergies, always contact the restaurant directly before ordering — we cannot guarantee cross-contamination control

**Note:** Allergen information is provided by the restaurant and may change without notice.

## Food Quality Complaints

### Problem: Food quality was poor, unsafe, or not as described

**Solution:**
1. For immediate safety concerns (foreign objects, food poisoning symptoms), contact support immediately
2. Report quality issues via **My Orders → [Order] → Report Issue → Food Quality** within 2 hours
3. Attach a photo if possible
4. We will:
   - Share your feedback with the restaurant
   - Issue appropriate compensation (partial/full refund or credits)
   - Flag the restaurant if multiple complaints are received

**Policy:** Repeated quality complaints may result in the restaurant being removed from our platform.

## Restaurant Ratings and Reviews

### Problem: Customer wants to review a restaurant or understand ratings

**Solution:**
1. After delivery, you will be prompted to rate the food and delivery experience
2. Ratings are on a 1-5 star scale for food quality, packaging, and accuracy
3. Leave a written review by tapping **Write a Review** in the order summary
4. Restaurant overall ratings are calculated from the last 90 days of orders
5. Unfair or abusive reviews may be removed by our moderation team

---

## Contacting a Restaurant Directly

### Problem: Customer wants to speak to the restaurant

**Solution:**
1. Open the restaurant page in the app
2. Tap **Restaurant Info → Contact Restaurant**
3. Use our anonymized calling system — your number is not shared
4. Restaurant contact is best for: special ingredient requests, allergy confirmations, customization questions
5. For order issues (missing items, wrong order), always report through the app — do not contact the restaurant directly for refunds
