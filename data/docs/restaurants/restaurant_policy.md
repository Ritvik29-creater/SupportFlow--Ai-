# SupportFlow AI — Restaurant Complaint Policy

## How Restaurant Ratings Work
- Every delivered order can be rated 1-5 stars
- Reviews are publicly visible on the restaurant's page
- Restaurants with consistent low ratings undergo a quality review
- Restaurants below 3.5 average rating for 30 days may be suspended

## Food Quality Complaints

### What qualifies as a food quality issue:
- Food arrived cold (temperature complaint)
- Food was undercooked or overcooked
- Wrong ingredients used (e.g., non-vegetarian item in vegetarian order)
- Allergen concerns (incorrect allergen information)
- Hygiene concerns (foreign object found in food, signs of contamination)
- Significantly different from menu description or photos
- Wrong spice level despite customization instructions

### How to report a food quality issue:
1. Do NOT discard the food — take a clear photo first
2. Report within 2 hours of delivery for fastest resolution
3. Go to Orders → Report Issue → Food Quality
4. Select specific items affected, upload photos
5. Choose your preferred resolution: refund, replacement, or wallet credit

## Restaurant Contact Information
- In-app "Call Restaurant" button available during active orders
- Restaurant direct number shown in order details after delivery
- Business hours for restaurant complaints: 9 AM - 11 PM IST

## Reporting a Restaurant for Serious Issues
If you encountered a serious issue such as:
- Severely unhygienic preparation
- Extremely rude behavior from restaurant staff
- Threats or harassment
- Confirmed food safety violation

You can escalate to our Restaurant Quality Team:
- Select "Serious Complaint" category when reporting
- A quality inspector may be assigned to visit the restaurant
- Restaurant may be temporarily suspended pending investigation
